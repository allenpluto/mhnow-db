The Hunting Horn's three styles, and the Shockwave mechanic solved
==================================================================
Dummy, 2026-11-23, Datura Horn 3: 1017 attack, 0% AFFINITY chosen
deliberately so damage is deterministic, element 364. Three recordings,
one per style. The engine models NONE of this - styleVariants() returns
a single unlabelled variant for the Hunting Horn and nothing prices the
melodies at all.

BUFF STATE IS MACHINE-READABLE. Three slots sit above the special
button; an activated one becomes a red-bordered icon where an inactive
one shows plain notes. Sampling that strip gives the buff count through
a whole recording without the player narrating it.


SHOCKWAVE: THE MECHANIC, SOLVED EXACTLY
---------------------------------------
The player's first description was that Shockwave "adds one shock wave
damage per buff when performing certain attacks". Close, but it missed
a step, and they corrected it after seeing the first numbers:

   THREE BUFFS ARE A PREREQUISITE, NOT THE TRIGGER. A jump shockwave
   attack must be performed to arm the state. Only then do following
   attacks spawn shockwaves.

The counter confirms it to the digit:

   t=48   781 damage / 4 HIT   =  463 + 106 x 3      1 real hit + 3 waves
   t=49  +1006        / +4     =  688 + 106 x 3
   t=52  + 106        / +1     <- a lone shockwave, no base hit at all

That third line is the cleanest evidence available: a shockwave landing
by itself, for exactly 106, counted as one hit.

   ONE SHOCKWAVE = 106 on this weapon, and the player has since tested
   that it SCALES WITH BOTH ATTACK AND ELEMENT. So it is an ordinary
   damage instance, not a flat bonus - which means its value as a
   PERCENTAGE should carry across weapons, rather than being worth more
   on a weak horn.

DURATION IS ABOUT TEN SECONDS. Reading the deltas through burst B5:
multi-hit groups run to t=55 and only single hits appear from t=57,
against an activation at roughly t=45 and the first shockwave at t=48.

IT RE-ARMS WITHOUT RE-BUFFING. Burst B8 opens at 781 / 4 HIT - the same
463 + 106x3 signature - after a re-activation at the end of B7, with no
new notes played. The player predicted this and the counter agrees.

WHAT IT IS WORTH
----------------
   base hits observed      463, 688, 476, 623, 860, 371, 835, 411, 569
                           mean 588
   during the state        588 -> 906 per hit        = +54%

   THE ACTIVATOR IS A BELOW-AVERAGE MOVE, NOT THE BEST ONE. It was first
   written up here as 993 dps and the highest burst of the eight, off a
   3-second window taken from the damage counter. The player then timed
   the move properly, 41.13 to 46.16s, and it is 5.03 SECONDS - the
   counter window had missed the wind-up and the recovery, which on this
   move are most of it.

      2978 damage over 5.03s = 592 dps
      the other seven bursts: 463, 641, 672, 700, 717, 789, 798

   So arming the state is second-lowest of the eight, and re-arming is
   NOT free. That removes the main reason to re-arm the instant it
   drops, and it cuts the best case badly:

   re-arm as soon as it drops   15.0s cycle  ->  915 dps  = +31%
   as actually played, 28s      28.0s cycle  ->  816 dps  = +17%

   (previously reported as +42% and +19%, on the free-activator error)

So Shockwave is worth roughly +17% as it was played and about +31%
played tightly. The gap is still pure play, but it is half what it
looked like, and the ceiling is a good deal lower.

   A CAUTION ON THE +54% PER HIT. It does not carry straight through to
   dps, because hit rate differs between moves and the shockwaves land
   on the same instant as their parent hit. The cycle figures above use
   an assumed 700 dps base from the unshockwaved bursts, which is the
   weakest number in this section.


WHAT THESE THREE VIDEOS CANNOT ANSWER
-------------------------------------
The styles cannot be compared from them, and the reason is structural
rather than a measurement problem.

   Shockwave        bursts at 641, 798, 717, 993, 672, 463, 789, 700 dps
   Fleeting Melody  bursts at 900, 645, 826, 1131, 678, 741 dps
   Normal           bursts at 635, 1119, 490, 719, 598, 606 dps

EVERY BURST IS A DIFFERENT MOVE. Within Shockwave alone the bursts
range from 463 to 993 dps - more than 2x - so the move matters more
than the style, and pooling them mixes the two. Buff count also drifts
inside several bursts.

One thing that IS visible: BUFF RETENTION DIFFERS. Fleeting Melody held
three buffs from t=10 to t=40, thirty unbroken seconds. Normal reached
three by t=10, lost them by t=30-35, sat at zero to t=42 and had to
rebuild. Whether that is the style or the player is not separable from
one run each, but it matches the player's description of Fleeting
Melody re-applying buffs whenever the right notes are played.

WHAT WOULD SETTLE IT, and it is much less work than mapping twenty
bursts to moves: ONE MOVE, repeated continuously for ten seconds or
more, at THREE BUFFS, once per style. Three short runs. The move must
be the same in all three and must not be the jump activator. That gives
one directly comparable number per style and nothing to disentangle.

A STANDING CAVEAT ON ALL OF IT: a dummy has no team, so Fleeting
Melody's entire team-buff half is worth exactly zero in this test while
Shockwave loses nothing it would not lose anyway. These figures are
PERSONAL damage, which is what the engine scores - they are not a
verdict on which style wins a four-player base defence.


FLEETING MELODY IS ITS OWN ARMED STATE, AND A BIGGER ONE
---------------------------------------------------------
Fleeting Melody turns out to work the same way Shockwave does - the
notes bar filling arms a state, and attacks landed while it is up are
worth more. The player timed one heavy attack on each side of it, and
because it is THE SAME MOVE WITH THE SAME HIT COUNT this is the
cleanest style measurement taken so far:

   32.24 - 38.18s  FM ACTIVE    5.94s   4524  4 hits   762 dps  1131/hit
   40.11 - 47.18s  FM expired   7.07s   2712  4 hits   384 dps   678/hit

   per hit   678 -> 1131   = +67%

THAT IS A BETTER NUMBER THAN ANYTHING IN THE SHOCKWAVE SECTION, because
it is one move against itself rather than a mean over several. The +54%
quoted for Shockwave was computed against an average base hit drawn
from different moves and deserves less weight than this.

DPS MOVED FURTHER THAN DAMAGE PER HIT - 384 to 762, +98% - because the
unbuffed window also ran 1.1 SECONDS LONGER for the same four hits.
Either Fleeting Melody speeds the attack up as well, or the two window
boundaries are not measuring quite the same thing. Worth one more pair
to separate, and until then the +67% per hit is the figure to trust.

IT ALSO LASTS FAR LONGER THAN SHOCKWAVE. The state armed at 15.13s and
had expired by somewhere between 38.18 and 40.11s:

   Fleeting Melody   about 23-25 seconds
   Shockwave         about 10 seconds, plus 5.03s of activator to arm it

SO ON PERSONAL DAMAGE ALONE, FLEETING MELODY CURRENTLY LOOKS BETTER
THAN SHOCKWAVE ON BOTH TERMS - a larger per-hit uplift held for more
than twice as long - and it buffs the team on top, which this dummy
scores at zero. That is the opposite of the advice the player was given
when they switched styles. One sample each, so it is a direction rather
than a verdict.

   the burst that straddles the 15.13s activation cannot help: the
   after-window is a single hit in under two seconds


CORRECTION: FLEETING MELODY IS +20%, NOT +67%
----------------------------------------------
The +67% above was wrong, and the player's own test replaced it. They
timed the same tap-tap-tap moves either side of the buff and recorded
each hit:

   without FM   463   609   238   794  1058
   with FM      556   730   286   952  1270
   ratio       1.2009 1.1987 1.2017 1.1990 1.2004

MEAN 1.2001, SPREAD 0.25%. At 0% affinity the damage is deterministic,
so five identical ratios are not a sample of the multiplier - they ARE
the multiplier. FLEETING MELODY IS EXACTLY +20% PER HIT.

WHY THE EARLIER FIGURES WERE INFLATED. The +67% came from a heavy-
attack pair whose "expired" window dropped from three melody buffs to
two partway through, so it was measuring Fleeting Melody PLUS a melody
buff. A second pair of normal attacks gave +38% and had the same
problem in reverse, two buffs against three. Neither held buff count
constant; the player's test did, by comparing single hits of the same
move.

   the lesson is not subtle: same move, same buff count, single hits.
   Windows and averages buried a clean 1.20 under confounds twice.


THE INDICATOR, AND WHAT IT SAYS ABOUT DURATION
-----------------------------------------------
Fleeting Melody has its own display, which the player pointed out: a
CURVED LINE OF SEVEN DOTS sitting above the three buff-activation
icons, which themselves sit above the special button.

   grey diamond   empty slot
   red ring       charged slot, filling toward seven
   ORANGE STAR    the state is ACTIVE, and these deplete

Counting strongly-orange pixels in that strip separates active from
charging without matching shapes, because the star's halo is far more
orange than a ring. Validated on video 2 against the player's own
15.13s activation.

   video 2: armed ~15.1s, stars gone by ~31.5s   =  16.4 SECONDS

THAT REVISES THE 23-25s ESTIMATE WRITTEN EARLIER, which was inferred
from damage falling off between two bursts rather than read off the
state itself.

AND IT IS NOT A PLAIN TIMER. The dot count runs 7, 6, 5, 3, 2, 0 across
successive samples - uneven steps. The player's explanation is that a
heavy attack consumes extra Fleeting Melody time, so the state is a
RESOURCE SPENT BY ATTACKING rather than a clock. Anything modelling its
uptime has to know what the player was doing with it.

   AND IT UNDERMINES THE +67% A SECOND WAY. That comparison called
   31.24-38.18s "FM active", but the stars were gone by about 31.5s -
   so most of that window was not under the buff at all.

ONE RECORDING COULD NOT BE USED: 14-25-17, a minute of tap-tap-tap
intended to measure attack SPEED, shows 0 damage and 0 HIT for its
entire length while the Fleeting Melody dots arm and deplete normally.
The player believes the counter bugged. The speed question - whether
the +20% damage is joined by a +20% attack rate, which would make the
buff 1.44x rather than 1.20x - is therefore still open, and it is the
last unknown on this style.


FLEETING MELODY, SETTLED: x1.20 DAMAGE, x1.12 SPEED, x1.35 DPS
----------------------------------------------------------------
A clean re-recording, 14-47-32, 73 seconds of nothing but the same
tap-tap-tap combo repeated eight times straight through a Fleeting
Melody window and out the other side. The combo turns out to be seven
hits in a fixed sequence, which makes every comparison here a like-for-
like one.

THE WHOLE COMBO CONFIRMS x1.20, not just the five hits timed by hand:

   no FM    FM     ratio
     463    556    1.2009
     609    730    1.1987
     794    952    1.1990
     238    286    1.2017
    1058   1270    1.2004
     397    476    1.1990

SIX DIFFERENT MOVES, EVERY ONE AT 1.20. At 0% affinity the damage is
deterministic, so this is the multiplier, not an estimate of it.

IT ALSO IDENTIFIES THE STATE WITHOUT READING THE DOTS. The sequence
463/609/794+238/1058/397/1587 is the unbuffed combo and 556/730/952+286
/1270/476 is the buffed one, so the damage values alone say which state
every hit was in. That is how combos 3 and 7 were confirmed to straddle
the activation exactly as the player described - first hit unbuffed,
everything after it buffed.

AND THE SPEED HALF IS REAL BUT SMALLER
--------------------------------------
Span from first hit to last hit of each combo - six intervals, seven
hits, measured at 20fps:

   no FM            7.20, 8.10, 7.25, 7.50   mean 7.51s
   FM               6.65, 6.60, 7.00, 6.50   mean 6.69s

   speed   x1.12    (12% faster)

   dps = 1.200 x 1.123 = x1.348

SO FLEETING MELODY IS WORTH ABOUT +35% DPS, not the +44% that a x1.20
speed bonus would have given. The player's theory - that the skill text
promising both attack and speed meant 1.2 x 1.2 - was right in shape
and high in size. The damage half is exactly 1.20; the speed half is
about 1.12.

   THE WEAKEST PART IS THE SPEED FIGURE. Four unbuffed combos against
   four buffed, and one unbuffed combo (8.10s) sits well outside the
   other three (7.20, 7.25, 7.50). Dropping it gives 10% rather than
   13%. So the speed bonus is somewhere near 10-13% and the dps figure
   near x1.33 to x1.36.

WHAT THIS MEANS NEXT TO SHOCKWAVE
---------------------------------
   Fleeting Melody   x1.35 dps, ~16.4s per activation
   Shockwave         +54% per hit, ~10s, after a 5.03s activator

Fleeting Melody is the weaker effect per second but holds for longer
and costs nothing to arm beyond notes the player is already playing.
Shockwave hits harder while up but spends five seconds of its worst
move getting there and lasts ten.

AND FLEETING MELODY BUFFS THE TEAM, which a dummy scores at zero. On
personal damage alone the two are closer than they look; with the team
counted, nothing in these recordings argues for Shockwave.
