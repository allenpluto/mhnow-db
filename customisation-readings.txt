The customisation track
=======================

What it is
----------
Customising a weapon runs a track of twenty steps: four stat steps then a
style selection, repeated four times. The four blocks are identical, so the
total at the end is four times one block. A weapon with no element takes four
attack steps; one with element alternates element, attack, element, attack.
Within a block the second occurrence of each stat is about one and a half
times the first. Nothing on the track ever touches affinity.

None of it is in the source. weaponLevels carries only levels 1 to 5 within a
grade, so all of this is read off weapon screens.

How to read a weapon screen
---------------------------
The black figure at the top is the SUM of the base value and everything added
since; the green figure in brackets is that addition. So

   base = black - green

and the result matches the database's own 10-5 figures on every weapon read,
which is a useful check that both are right.

The green bracket holds TWO things added together: the customisation track
and the three stat slots. Despot's Blitz shows element +420, which is 120
from its track plus 300 from three slots of +100. Skullwhistle shows affinity
+30%, which is three slots of +10% and nothing else, the track never touching
affinity.

THE RULE: THE TRACK FOLLOWS THE STAT LINE
------------------------------------------
Not the monster, not the weapon type, not the element. Seven lines were read
on more than one monster and every one agrees:

   1817/516     Sinister, Bishaten, Rathian          all 100/40
   1463/1420    Rathalos, Halloween, Legiana         all 80/80
   2113/0       Paolumu, Bishaten, Banbaro           all 392/0

   1912/417     Halloween, Pukei, Radobaan           all 100/40

Element type does not enter into it: Blast against Paralysis on the first

The two exceptions
------------------
ZINOGRE gives 120/120 where Rathalos, Halloween and Legiana all give 80/80 on
the same line. Legiana was the deciding reading and came down against it.
Nothing in the data marks Zinogre out, so it is listed rather than explained.

THE HOPE SET is a different kind of exception. Its customisation stops at
level 5 - one block rather than four - so it grants 38/0 in total and never
reaches the three stat slots at all. It shares the 2251/0 line with Sinister
and Rathian weapons, which do run the full track and do get 204/0. The
suggester turns the stat-slots option off for it and says why.

There is no formula, and that is settled
-----------------------------------------
Everything reasonable was tried and failed:

   weapon type        four pairs sharing base stats across types match
   base attack        Bazelhawk 2327 and Snow Sisters 1903 take the same
                      steps; Illusory at 1878 takes less than Snow Sisters
                      at 1903
   percentage of anything   of base attack 4.7% to 18.6%, of attack plus
                      element 1.39% to 2.46%, of the 10-1 figures 1.41% to
                      3.02%, of the growth between grades 0.071 to 0.152
   grade span         weapons spanning grades 5-10 run from 1.17% to 1.98%
   smooth banding     2103 and 2113 are TEN APART and give 352 and 392,
                      while 2251, 2371 and 2658 all give 204
   the number after the weapon name   it counts position within a name block,
                      Girros Hammer 1-4 for grades 2-5 then Malady's Fist 1-5
                      for grades 6-10, so it carries nothing the grade does
                      not

Paolumu makes the point on its own: base 2113 draws 392 where Black Diablos
at 2658 draws 204. A weaker weapon taking nearly double looks like a number
set by hand to prop up the earlier sets, and the table above is simply how to
look it up.

In the suggester
----------------
Ticking "spend the three customisation stat slots" fills the attack and
element boxes from this table, a weapon customised far enough to have those
slots being at the end of its track. Unticking takes the figures back out,
but only the ones it put in, so a number typed by hand survives, and changing
weapon re-fills for the new one. The panel under the weapon dropdown states
the figure whether or not the box is ticked, which is how a Hope Set weapon
reports its 38 with no box to tick.


TWO READINGS FROM THE EQUIPMENT-LIST POPUP, 2026-11-12
-------------------------------------------------------
These came off the compact popup in the Equipment list - the one with no
weapon image and no green bracket - rather than the full weapon screen the
rest of this file was read from. So they give the black figure only, and the
addition has to be taken as the difference against the database.

   weapon         database 10-5      popup              difference
   Nifl Mist      1363/1436/20%      1411/1508/20%      +48/+72/0%
   Frost Blitz    1463/1420/0%       1483/1448/0%       +20/+28/0%

AFFINITY MATCHES EXACTLY ON BOTH, which confirms the database base on both
weapons and says neither had an affinity stat slot. Both differences are too
small for a stat slot of any kind (+100/+100/+10%), so both are partially
run customisation tracks and nothing else.

Nifl Mist's line, 1363/1436, is the Mizutsune line, whose full track is
recorded above as 64/96. +48/+72 is exactly 75% of that in BOTH stats, which
fits three of the four blocks run and is a neat independent check on the
table.

Frost Blitz's line, 1463/1420, is recorded above as 80/80. +20/+28 is not a
clean fraction of that in both stats at once, so it is a track stopped
part-way through a block rather than on a block boundary.

AN OPEN QUESTION THIS RAISES, and it matters more than the two readings do.
The player reports that this popup shows the FINAL stat, including static
skill boosts such as Ice Attack and raw attack, excluding only dynamic ones
like Vital Ice and Peak Performance. The method recorded above says the
opposite - that black minus green is the database base, with the green
bracket holding only the track and the stat slots, no skills.

Both builds above carry ATTACK BOOST 2, which this engine books at +100
attack. The entire attack difference is +20 on Frost Blitz and +48 on Nifl
Mist. So one of two things is true and they cannot both be:

   either the popup does NOT include skills, and these differences are pure
      customisation - which is what the rest of this file assumes
   or it DOES, and ATTACK_BOOST = {1:50, 2:100, 3:150, 4:200, 5:300} is badly
      wrong, since Attack Boost 2 alone would have to fit inside +20

The two screenshots cannot separate them, because both builds carry the same
Attack Boost level, so the skill and the track are confounded. ONE SCREENSHOT
SETTLES IT: the same weapon's popup with the armour swapped for a set at a
different Attack Boost level. If the figure moves, the popup includes skills
and the attack table needs rebuilding; if it does not, it is the track alone
and nothing here changes.

SETTLED, the same day, by two screenshots of ONE build on BOTH screens.

Nifl Mist 5 wearing Water Attack 3, Raw Power 3, Defense Boost 2:

                   weapon popup    Status screen    difference
      attack          1411            1711            +300
      element         1508            1708            +200
      affinity          20%              6%            -14%

THE WEAPON POPUP DOES NOT INCLUDE SKILLS. It reads 1411/20%/1508 with those
three skills equipped, which is exactly what it read without them. The
no-skills reading recorded in this file is correct and the attack table is
not in question. The Status screen is the one that totals everything.

SO THERE ARE TWO SCREENS AND THEY ANSWER DIFFERENT QUESTIONS:
   the weapon popup    base + customisation track + stat slots
   the Status screen   that, plus every static skill

Three constants fell out of the difference, and this is the first time any of
them has been checked against the game rather than assumed:
   Raw Power 3       +300 attack        confirmed exactly
   Water Attack 3    +200 element       confirmed exactly
   Raw Power 3       -14% affinity      ... but see below

A DRIFTSMELT SLOT CARRIES AN EXTRA STAT BONUS ON TOP OF ITS SKILL
------------------------------------------------------------------
Attack 1-20, OR defence 1-20, OR affinity 1-2%, per slot. It is not a skill
and belongs to no skill, and it lands in the Status screen.

That is the whole of the -14%: Raw Power's -15% plus +1% from a drift slot.
The table was right and the "correction" to 14 was a confound, now reverted.

THE ATTACK COLUMN PROVES IT rather than merely allowing it. It comes to +300
EXACTLY. Had a slot rolled attack instead of affinity the total would have
been 301 to 320. So on this build the drift bonus is affinity, which leaves
Raw Power's attack clean and explains the single point of affinity. Element is
untouched either way, since no drift bonus rolls element.

THE SIZE OF IT. Five slots at up to 20 attack is +100 attack, or at up to 2%
each, +10% affinity - neither of which this engine prices. It models a drift
slot only as the skill it grants, which --gems assumes is ideal. The stat
bonus is rolled, not chosen, so it is not something a build can be optimised
around; but it is real, and on a fully slotted set it is not small.

THE LESSON FOR EVERY FUTURE READING OFF THE STATUS SCREEN: measure constants
on a build with EMPTY driftsmelt slots, or expect to be out by exactly this
much. This one nearly wrote a wrong number into the engine.

One caveat on the weapon popup, which does not undermine any of the above but
is worth knowing: it DOES reflect skills in the RECOIL AND RELOAD TIERS, even
though it does not reflect them in attack, element or affinity. The same
weapon's Water Ammo reads Low under Recoil Down 2 and Average without it,
which is the step the engine already models. So the popup is skill-free for
the three stats and skill-aware for the two tiers.
