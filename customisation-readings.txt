The customisation track
=======================

What it is
----------
Customising a weapon runs a track of twenty steps: four stat steps then a
style selection, repeated four times. The four blocks are identical, so the
total at the end is four times one block. A weapon with no element takes four
attack steps; one with element alternates element, attack, element, attack.
Within a block the second occurrence of each stat is about one and a half
times the first. Nothing on the track ever touches affinity.

None of it is in the source. weaponLevels carries only levels 1 to 5 within a
grade, so all of this is read off weapon screens.

How to read a weapon screen
---------------------------
The black figure at the top is the SUM of the base value and everything added
since; the green figure in brackets is that addition. So

   base = black - green

and the result matches the database's own 10-5 figures on every weapon read,
which is a useful check that both are right.

The green bracket holds TWO things added together: the customisation track
and the three stat slots. Despot's Blitz shows element +420, which is 120
from its track plus 300 from three slots of +100. Skullwhistle shows affinity
+30%, which is three slots of +10% and nothing else, the track never touching
affinity.

THE RULE: THE TRACK FOLLOWS THE STAT LINE
------------------------------------------
Not the monster, not the weapon type, not the element. Seven lines were read
on more than one monster and every one agrees:

   1817/516     Sinister, Bishaten, Rathian          all 100/40
   1463/1420    Rathalos, Halloween, Legiana         all 80/80
   2113/0       Paolumu, Bishaten, Banbaro           all 392/0
   1579/1568    Bazelgeuse, Deviljho, Glavenus       all 128/100
   2103/0       Barroth, Pukei, Radobaan             all 352/0
   1912/417     Halloween, Pukei, Radobaan           all 100/40
   2251/0       Sinister, Rathian                    both 204/0

Element type does not enter into it: Blast against Paralysis on the first
line, Fire against Dragon on the fourth.

The two exceptions
------------------
ZINOGRE gives 120/120 where Rathalos, Halloween and Legiana all give 80/80 on
the same line. Legiana was the deciding reading and came down against it.
Nothing in the data marks Zinogre out, so it is listed rather than explained.

THE HOPE SET is a different kind of exception. Its customisation stops at
level 5 - one block rather than four - so it grants 38/0 in total and never
reaches the three stat slots at all. It shares the 2251/0 line with Sinister
and Rathian weapons, which do run the full track and do get 204/0. The
suggester turns the stat-slots option off for it and says why.

There is no formula, and that is settled
-----------------------------------------
Everything reasonable was tried and failed:

   weapon type        four pairs sharing base stats across types match
   base attack        Bazelhawk 2327 and Snow Sisters 1903 take the same
                      steps; Illusory at 1878 takes less than Snow Sisters
                      at 1903
   percentage of anything   of base attack 4.7% to 18.6%, of attack plus
                      element 1.39% to 2.46%, of the 10-1 figures 1.41% to
                      3.02%, of the growth between grades 0.071 to 0.152
   grade span         weapons spanning grades 5-10 run from 1.17% to 1.98%
   smooth banding     2103 and 2113 are TEN APART and give 352 and 392,
                      while 2251, 2371 and 2658 all give 204
   the number after the weapon name   it counts position within a name block,
                      Girros Hammer 1-4 for grades 2-5 then Malady's Fist 1-5
                      for grades 6-10, so it carries nothing the grade does
                      not

Paolumu makes the point on its own: base 2113 draws 392 where Black Diablos
at 2658 draws 204. A weaker weapon taking nearly double looks like a number
set by hand to prop up the earlier sets, and the table above is simply how to
look it up.

In the suggester
----------------
Ticking "spend the three customisation stat slots" fills the attack and
element boxes from this table, a weapon customised far enough to have those
slots being at the end of its track. Unticking takes the figures back out,
but only the ones it put in, so a number typed by hand survives, and changing
weapon re-fills for the new one. The panel under the weapon dropdown states
the figure whether or not the box is ticked, which is how a Hope Set weapon
reports its 38 with no box to tick.
