Reading skill procs off the banner, and what it did to Burst
============================================================
Every skill that procs puts a banner at the top left of the screen for about
two seconds: the skill's name and its hexagonal icon on a dark pill. The pill
grows to fit the name, so the icon sits further right for a longer skill.

THAT MAKES IT MACHINE-READABLE. The text and icon render identically every
time, so a template of the bright pixels - thresholded, then compared by
intersection-over-union against every frame at 2fps - finds a named skill's
procs across a whole recording in about a second of compute, whatever the
scenery behind it. The previous approach was to find a frame where the banner
happened to be legible against sky, which is why the constants below rested
on two readings instead of six.

VALIDATION BEFORE USE, both directions:
   the Combo Master template, run on the one build with NO Combo Master in it
      (17Nov 21-10-49, the Valor set), returns NOTHING
   the Combo Master template, run on a fight with a known proc, returns that
      proc at a match of 0.95 and nothing else - so it is rejecting Valor,
      Burst, Shared Sword, Divine Protection and the rest, not just finding
      any banner


COMBO MASTER FIRES ONCE PER FIGHT. NEVER TWICE.
===============================================
The question was whether it repeats like Burst or fires once like Valor.
Across nine recordings carrying the skill, every single one shows EXACTLY ONE
Combo Master banner - while Burst banners five to seven times in those same
fights as its chain breaks and re-arms.

So the name was a false friend. The source calls it POWER_BURST and the
engine priced it at Burst's uptime on that basis; it is not a Burst.

   recording        lv   fight start   Combo Master   into the fight
   17Nov 21-02-13    3        5s           17.0s           12.0s
   17Nov 21-04-56    3       30s           46.0s           16.0s
   17Nov 21-18-48    3       14s           33.5s           19.5s
   17Nov 12-09-26    3       17s           40.5s           23.5s
   16Nov 18-07-09    ?       13s           28.5s           15.5s
   17Nov 21-14-09    2       62s          NEVER               -

Twelve to twenty-four seconds in, once, and then nothing for the rest of the
fight. Taken with the absence of any second banner, the reading is that it
arms once and holds - the same shape as Valor.

THAT IS AN INFERENCE, AND IT IS THE WEAK POINT HERE. The banner says when the
skill armed; it does not say the buff is still up at the end. What rules out
the alternative - that it expired and simply never re-armed - is that the
condition would have to become unmeetable for ninety seconds of continuous
fighting, and Burst's banner shows the player was landing chains throughout.

AND ONE FIGHT IT NEVER FIRED AT ALL. 17Nov 21-14-09 is 109 seconds of Heavy
Bowgun with Combo Master 2 equipped and not one banner, confirmed at a
loosened threshold so it is absence rather than a detection failure.
"Performing an action multiple times" is evidently a melee combo that a gun
may never perform. One sample cannot justify making the skill weapon
specific, so the misfire is carried in the average as risk instead: 0.666
with it, 0.832 without, and the lower is taken.


BURST: THE 0.50 WAS LOW BY TWO THIRDS
=====================================
Burst's uptime is set by its first activation - the chain analysis already in
the engine shows it barely ever breaks once started, with ceilings of 87% to
100%. The constant rested on two Charge Blade runs and took the lower.

Six fights, read by template, fight boundaries from the damage counter:

   recording        lv    fight       first Burst   uptime
   17Nov 12-09-26    5   17-122s         28.0s       0.895
   17Nov 21-02-13    5    5-104s         27.5s       0.781
   17Nov 21-04-56    5   30-134s         38.5s       0.918
   17Nov 21-18-48    5   14-121s         24.0s       0.907
   17Nov 21-14-09    2   62-171s         71.0s       0.917
   17Nov 21-10-49    1   24-129s         82.0s       0.448

   mean 0.811, and five of six sit between 0.78 and 0.92

All six are under the 87-100% ceilings the chain analysis puts on them, which
is the consistency check worth having: two independent methods on different
recordings, and the first-activation numbers do not contradict the lull
numbers.

THE 0.448 STAYS IN. That is the Switch Axe run where the player reports
missing repeatedly, and the damage counter agrees - 2067/s against 2903/s on
the same weapon two fights earlier. Burst took 58 seconds to arm because the
hits were not landing. A real outcome of a real hunt, not an error, and it
drags the mean from 0.87 to 0.81. The spread is the skill: Burst pays you for
connecting, so a build around it is worth less to a player having a bad day.

   burstUptime        0.50 -> 0.81
   comboMasterUptime  new, 0.67   (was sharing burstUptime)

Burst 5 goes from +15% to +24%, Combo Master 3 from +17.5% to +23.5%.


THE PART THAT WASN'T CIRCULAR
=============================
These uptimes came from banner timings. They have nothing to do with the
damage totals on the result screen. So what happened next is a check rather
than a fit:

   Switch Axe, Burst build against Valor build, same weapon, 104s
      before   VALOR 5188   BURST 4822     engine preferred VALOR by 7.6%
      after    BURST 5638   VALOR 5267     engine prefers BURST by 6.6%

   measured on the day   BURST 2903 and 2126/s   VALOR 2067/s

The engine's preference flipped to the side that actually won, on constants
derived from a completely separate signal. That is not proof - the measured
gap is inside the +/-30% run-to-run spread, so the fights never settled it -
but a model that disagreed with the only two build comparisons on record now
agrees with both.

UNCHANGED BY ALL OF THIS: Valor 2 still beats Skyward Striker 2 on the
Thanatos Glaive, by 4.2% at 105s rather than the earlier 4.7%.

WHAT THIS METHOD COULD DO NEXT, now that it exists:
   - Latent Power, Rising Tide and anything else with a ramp - the banner
     gives the start, which is the whole constant for a hold-to-end skill
   - whether Combo Master's misfire is a Heavy Bowgun property or a one-off,
     which needs one more bowgun fight with the skill equipped
   - Burst's first activation on a fast weapon against a slow one, which is
     the extrapolation the old comment flagged and this still has not tested
