The slicing evade loop: stepping shot + Evading Reload + Slicing Ammo Boost
===========================================================================

The playstyle, as described by the player
-----------------------------------------
Light Bowgun, style-selectable, carrying slicing. Open on normal or pierce,
switch to the slicing magazine, and then NEVER actively reload again. Keep
dodging to shoot, and when the magazine runs dry the dodges themselves have a
chance to reload it.

The suggester does not know this build exists, and understates it by roughly
two and a half to three times. The reason is not a wrong number anywhere - it
is that the engine prices three things separately that are ONE EVENT in play.

The identity the engine is missing
----------------------------------
UNDER STEPPING SHOT, THE SHOT IS THE DODGE. Stepping shot replaces the shot
with a dodge and fires at a fixed 0.975s whatever the recoil - measured. And
Evading Reload triggers on a dodge. So every shot rolls Evading Reload, and
the trigger rate is not merely high, it is the highest it can physically be:
one roll per 0.975s, for free, with no action spent on it.

This is the same shape as the Fighting Spirit / Elemental Release identity
already in the engine - two skills keyed to the same instant - and it is
handled the same way: derive it from the timeline instead of guessing.

The engine instead holds:
   - stepping shot's cycle as (capacity - 1) dodges PLUS a real reload off the
     reload ladder
   - Evading Reload as a flat 4/7/10/13/15% of damage, a guess that carries a
     comment saying it is a guess
   - Slicing Ammo Boost at 1/n of its value, n being the number of magazines,
     because stepping shot is assumed to fire its magazines evenly

All three are wrong for this build, and they are wrong in the same direction.

The arithmetic, from the measured dodge
---------------------------------------
Treat Evading Reload as a chance p of refilling the magazine on each dodge,
which is how the player describes it. In steady state each dodge consumes one
round and supplies p x cap rounds, so

      SUSTAINABLE  <=>  p x cap >= 1  <=>  p >= 1 / cap

Above that threshold the magazine never runs dry, no reload is ever taken, and
EVERY gap in the cycle is one dodge - the reload gaps included. The cadence is
therefore the dodge's own:

      CEILING = 1 / 0.975 = 1.026 rounds per second

AND IT DOES NOT DEPEND ON MAGAZINE SIZE. That is the whole trick. A 2-round
slicing magazine fires exactly as fast as a 10-round one, because capacity
only ever mattered as the thing that set how often you paid a reload.

Below the threshold, fire is supply-limited at p x cap rounds per dodge, and a
player would reload by hand instead of dodging on an empty gun - so the real
rate is the better of the two.

The threshold is low, because slicing magazines are small
---------------------------------------------------------
   Devil's Madness, Glavenus Blitz, Frost Blitz, Nifl Mist,
   Rathbuster, Lightning Blitz      3 slicing rounds   needs p >= 33%
   Rose Assault                     2 slicing rounds   needs p >= 50%

Rounds per second on the slicing magazine, against reloading by hand, where
* marks the evade loop being ahead:

   3-round magazines   native 0.405   stepping shot alone 0.571
      p = 10%   0.571     20%   0.615*    30%   0.923*
      p = 15%   0.571     25%   0.769*    33%+  1.026*  ceiling
   Rose Assault        native 0.408   stepping shot alone 0.523
      p = 25%   0.523     33%   0.684*    50%+  1.026*  ceiling
      p = 30%   0.615*    40%   0.821*

What it is worth, at the ceiling, on the slicing magazine:

   against no style, reloading normally        x2.52
   against stepping shot alone                x1.79  (x1.96 on Rose Assault)

SPARE SHOT IS THE THRESHOLD SKILL, AND RELOAD SPEED IS DEAD
-----------------------------------------------------------
This is the part that changes what to wear, and it inverts the suggester's
current advice.

A round Spare Shot saves is a round the refill does not have to supply, so
demand per dodge is (1 - spare chance) and the condition becomes

      p x cap >= 1 - spare

                     refill chance needed to sustain endless fire
   magazine    Spare 0   Spare 1   Spare 2   Spare 3
   2 rounds        50%       45%       40%       35%
   3 rounds        33%       30%       27%       23%

So Spare Shot is the skill that gets you OVER the threshold - and is worth
exactly nothing once you are over it. Reload Speed is the precise opposite: it
shortens a reload this build never takes. Recoil Down was already worth
nothing under stepping shot, which the engine does know.

The suggester's current answer for Rose Assault at 75s is
   Reload Speed 3, Spare Shot 1, Slicing Ammo Boost 2
Reload Speed 3 is the single largest piece of that and it is dead weight for
this playstyle, while Slicing Ammo Boost is left at 2 because the engine
believes half its value leaks into the pierce magazine.

Two thirds of that is the firing rate and one third is the ammo boost coming

its full +100% instead of +50% on a two-magazine gun or +33% on a three.

WHAT IS NOT KNOWN, AND THE ONE MEASUREMENT THAT SETTLES IT
----------------------------------------------------------
p. Everything above is a function of the refill chance per dodge, and that
chance has never been measured - it is the open item already recorded in
recoil-readings.txt, where the note says an evade's refill was never caught on
video. What has changed is that the OTHER half of that open item is now
answered: "how often is an evade available" is no longer unknown, because
under stepping shot an evade is available on literally every shot.

One recording settles it, by the method already used for the recoil ladder:
stepping shot on a slicing magazine, firing continuously, decoding the hit
counter frame by frame. The share of dodges that produce a hit IS p x cap
directly, with no modelling in between. A run long enough to see twenty
reloads would pin p to a few percent.

Until then the honest statement is a conditional, not a number: IF Evading
Reload's refill chance reaches 1/capacity - 33% on six of these seven weapons,
and less with Spare Shot - then the loop fires one slicing round every 0.975s
indefinitely, and the build is worth about x5 the bare weapon where the
suggester books about x1.8.

And one thing this cannot say
-----------------------------
An absolute damage-per-second figure. Nothing in this project has ever
measured a bowgun's damage per round - the recoil work timed the hit counter,
not the damage panel, and the only dmg/s measurements on file are Dual Blades.
So the rate is exact and the multipliers are exact, but turning x5.03 into a
number of damage per second would need a dummy recording of the slicing
magazine, which has not been taken.
