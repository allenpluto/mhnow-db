Special Gauge and Elemental Release, measured from video
========================================================

Why it matters
--------------
Elemental Release is not a free-floating uptime. The skill reads "elemental
attack power increases by 100% for 20 secs. after activating a Special Skill",
so the uptime is 20 seconds times how many Specials a hunt yields, over its
length. Everything below is about counting Specials.

Method
------
The Special button is tracked frame by frame off recordings resampled to a
constant 20fps, by counting the warm-coloured pixels of the wedge filling it.
Firing the Special empties the button, which shows as a sustained full reading
followed by a fall.

What the HUD actually does, learned the hard way
------------------------------------------------
   - The button is a DIFFERENT SIZE for different weapons, on the same device
     and the same recorder: radius about 21px on an Insect Glaive, about 43px
     on Dual Blades. It has to be located per recording, not assumed.
   - On a training dummy the fill reads yellow; in a real hunt it reads
     orange. One colour test will not do for both.
   - The pre-fight start button is the same colour in nearly the same place,
     which reads as a Special fired before the fight has begun.
   - DUAL BLADES use the same button for two things. One press enters Demon
     Mode; a second press while the gauge is full fires the Special. A red
     swirl on the button means Demon Mode is running and the gauge is NOT
     full, so long red stretches are not a stolen or empty gauge.
   - Also on Dual Blades, the yellow bar under the health bar is Demon Mode's
     own energy, which drains while Demon Mode is up. POWER PROLONGER SLOWS
     THAT DRAIN. That makes Power Prolonger measurable - see the open items.

How fast the gauge fills
------------------------
Two training-dummy runs, attacking without pause:

   Charge Blade,  attack 1450    39.1s and 40.9s between Specials
   Insect Glaive, attack 1155    42.0s and 33.3s

Two weapons 26% apart on attack and TWICE apart on hit rate - 1.2 against 2.5
hits a second - refilled in near enough the same wall-clock time. So the gauge
fills on TIME SPENT ATTACKING, not on damage dealt and not on hits landed.
About 38 seconds of uninterrupted attacking buys one Special.

One real hunt, same Insect Glaive, solo against Rajang: Special fired at 6.9s,
gauge back to 67% by 41.4s, so a full refill would have taken 51.6s. About a
third longer than the dummy, which is the price of not attacking continuously.
The engine uses 52s.

A real hunt end to end
----------------------
Dual Blades, three players, 5-star Riftborne Deviljho, with Valor 3 and
Special Partbreaker 5. The hunt timer read 60 at video t=40, so it began at
t=25 and ran the standard 75 seconds. Specials fired at video t = 34, 47 and
68, which is 9s, 22s and 43s into the hunt.

   windows   9-29, 22-42, 43-63
   merged    9-42, 43-63
   up for    53s of 75s = 70.7%

The engine computes 80% for that hunt - three Specials times 20 seconds over
75 - because it assumes the windows do not overlap. Here the first two were
fired 13 seconds apart, so 7 seconds of the second window fell inside the
first and bought nothing.

So the structure is right and the arithmetic is optimistic by exactly the
overlap. The cause is not the game but the player: firing a Special the moment
the gauge fills wastes Elemental Release when a previous window is still
running. Worth knowing as play advice, and worth not over-fitting a correction
to from one hunt.

Valor and Special Partbreaker both hand over gauge outright rather than
earning it, which is what let three Specials fire in a 75-second hunt when the
natural refill alone would have given two.

What this leaves
----------------
   40s hunt    50%        these are what the engine now uses, against the
   75s hunt    53%        flat 35% that was assumed before any of it was
   150s hunt   40%        measured

With Valor adding its free Special: 100%, 80% and 53%. With Nergigante
Avidity, which stops the gauge filling from attacking at all: 50%, 27% and
13%, or 100%, 53% and 27% if Valor is also carried.

Open items
----------
   - POWER PROLONGER is still a flat 5/10/15% guess across seven weapon
     types. On Dual Blades it is now known to slow the Demon Mode energy
     drain, and that bar is on screen under the health bar. Timing Demon Mode
     from entry to collapse at Power Prolonger 0 and again at 3 would turn
     that guess into a measurement, on the weapon where the skill matters
     most.
   - BURST is a flat 35% guess. The skill states its own duration, "+30%
     attack for 4 seconds" after consecutive hits, so only the trigger rate
     needs reading, and the banner that announces it is on screen. Footage
     carrying Burst 5 already exists.
   - Specials fired close together waste uptime, and the engine does not
     model it. One hunt is not enough to say how much is typically wasted.
