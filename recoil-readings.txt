Shot timings read off gameplay video, 2026-11-09
================================================

Method
------
Three screen recordings of a training dummy (480x1046, variable frame rate,
nominally 60fps but really about 26.5). Frame counts are therefore useless for
timing, so each recording was resampled to a constant 20fps and the "n HIT"
counter in the Total Damage panel was decoded frame by frame: the region
x=270..360, y=220..254 was differenced against the previous frame, and a change
of more than a dozen bright pixels taken as the counter moving. That times every
hit to 0.05s.

A hit is not a shot. Pierce and slicing rounds land several times per shot, so
shots are the first hit of each cluster; within a cluster the hits are 0.10s
apart, between shots they are 0.6-1.1s apart, so the two never run together.

     clusters beginning 13.45, 15.50, 18.15, each five hits over about a
     second. One round hitting repeatedly, so where one shot ends and the next

Result
------
   Low        0.633s between shots   (3 gaps, one weapon)
   Average    0.865s                 (10 gaps, two weapons, agreeing to 0.003s)
   High       1.057s                 (7 gaps, two weapons, agreeing to 0.005s)
   Very high  not measured
   LARGE_2    not measured, and never seen as its own word on a weapon screen

Two different weapons agreeing to within 0.005s means these are the tiers
themselves rather than anything about the weapons.

What this says about Recoil Down
--------------------------------
Timed by hand earlier on Anja Buster (flaming rounds, Average recoil):

   without the skill   8 shots in 5.90s over 7 gaps  = 0.843s
   with Recoil Down 2  6 shots in 3.23s over 5 gaps  = 0.646s

0.843 against the Average tier's 0.865, and 0.646 against the Low tier's 0.633
- so Recoil Down 2 is one whole tier, within the error of a stopwatch.

STILL OPEN: whether Lv1 is half a tier or a whole one. If the skill moves one
tier per level then Lv1 already does everything Lv2 does on an Average gun and
the higher levels are dead weight there; if it scales evenly, Lv1 is worth
about half. One timing of a full magazine at Recoil Down 1 on an
Average-recoil gun decides it - near 0.63s means per-tier, near 0.74s means
even scaling. The engine currently assumes even scaling.

Reload was never timed. The player repositions between magazines in all three
recordings, so no gap in them is a clean reload.
