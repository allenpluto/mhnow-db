Standardised dummy DPS, for comparing weapons and styles
=========================================================
A running table of dummy measurements normalised by weapon attack, so
figures taken on different weapons can be set side by side. NOT used by
the engine - this is a reference for judging future measurements.

HOW TO ADD A ROW. Dummy, 0% AFFINITY so the damage is deterministic,
attack continuously for about a minute, and take the span from the
first damage to the last with the panel's idle reset excluded. State
the weapon's attack, element and affinity. That method is one recording
and needs no phase bookkeeping, and it is what the Hunting Horn rows
below use.

   dps/atk = dps / attack            the figure to compare
   dps/ae  = dps / (attack+element)  the other bound, see the caveats

=== WHY dps/atk IS NOT A CLEAN CLASS COMPARISON ===

ELEMENT. The Datura Horn carries 364 element against 1017 attack, so a
large share of its damage is elemental and a no-element horn will not
reproduce 0.765. The two Switch Axe and Charge Blade weapons have no
element at all. dps/ae is the other bound; the truth is between, and
separating them needs one more weapon with a different element split.

AFFINITY. Thanatos Force sits at -30% affinity, so its 0.951 is
achieved DESPITE a penalty the other weapons do not carry. Corrected
upward it would be higher still, which widens rather than narrows the
Charge Blade's lead.

ATTACK SCALING MAY NOT BE LINEAR. Every row here comes from one weapon
per class, so dividing by attack assumes damage is proportional to it.
Nothing in this project has tested that. Two weapons of the same class
at different attack values would settle it and should be the next thing
added.

WHAT THE TABLE IS GOOD FOR AS IT STANDS: comparing STYLES within one
weapon, where everything else is held constant and the numbers are
exact. The Hunting Horn block and the Switch Axe block are each
internally sound. Comparing ACROSS blocks is not yet supported.
