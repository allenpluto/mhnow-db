Standardised dummy DPS, for comparing weapons and styles
=========================================================
A running table of dummy measurements normalised by weapon attack, so
figures taken on different weapons can be set side by side. NOT used by
the engine - a reference for judging future measurements.

A FLAT ONE-MINUTE NUMBER IS NOT ENOUGH, which is the main thing this
file exists to say. Most weapons cycle between a weak state and a
strong one, often with dead time to transform between them, and a
single blended figure throws that structure away. The structure is
exactly what decides whether an uptime skill like Power Prolonger is
worth a slot.

SO EACH ROW RECORDS FOUR THINGS, not one:

   down     dps while the state is down (charging, normal, sword...)
   up       dps while it is up (boosted, demon, axe boost...)
   up dur   how long the state lasts once armed
   cycle    down duration + activation + up duration

   blended  = computed from those, and is the comparable figure
   dps/atk  = blended / attack

ONE CONTINUOUS RECORDING CAN GIVE ALL OF IT, which is the cheap part:
attack for about a minute on a 0% AFFINITY weapon so damage is
deterministic, then split the recording by state afterwards. The
Fleeting Melody row below was taken that way - the same recording gave
570 down, 927 up and the 726 blended.

=== WHAT THE DECOMPOSITION IS FOR ===

Pricing an uptime skill needs the up/down RATIO and the CURRENT UPTIME
together - neither alone is enough. Applying Power Prolonger 3's
measured 2.50x window extension to each row above:

   class          ratio  uptime -> uptime   gain
   Charge Blade   3.55x    76%  ->  89%    +11.3%
   Hunting Horn   1.63x    46%  ->  68%    +10.7%
   Switch Axe     1.51x    50%  ->  71%     +8.6%

THEY LAND WITHIN THREE POINTS OF EACH OTHER DESPITE RATIOS FROM 1.5x TO
3.6x, because a weapon with a strong up-state tends to already spend
most of its cycle there, and the two effects partly cancel. The engine
carries POWER_PROLONGER[3] = 0.120 flat for all seven weapon types and
the Charge Blade measured +12.5%; this is why a single constant has
survived, and it is a better reason than the one originally given.

   the decomposition would matter much more for a weapon that breaks
   the pattern - a strong up-state held only briefly, or a weak one
   held nearly always. None has turned up yet. Finding one is the
   reason to keep recording the split rather than just the total.


=== WHY dps/atk IS NOT A CLEAN CLASS COMPARISON ===

ELEMENT. Datura Horn carries 364 element against 1017 attack, so much
of its damage is elemental; the Switch Axe and Charge Blade weapons
have none. A no-element horn will not reproduce 0.765.

AFFINITY. Thanatos Force sits at -30%, so its 0.969 is achieved DESPITE
a penalty the others do not carry. Correcting for it widens the Charge
Blade's lead rather than narrowing it.

ATTACK SCALING MAY NOT BE LINEAR. One weapon per class, so dividing by
attack assumes proportionality nothing here has tested. Two weapons of
the same class at different attack values would settle it and are the
most valuable rows to add next.
