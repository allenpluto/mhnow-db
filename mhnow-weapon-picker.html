Derived columns — from the maxed 10-5 stats. Affinity and Critical Boost apply to attack and element, so every build below is computed off Sum. Each column is a whole-hit damage figure — they are alternative builds to compare, not parts to add up.
Each build's final affinity is worked out first, because that decides whether its skill applies at all: Critical Boost and Critical Element only when final affinity > 0, Critical Ferocity only when < 0. Outside its range a column shows — rather than a meaningless number.
Sum = attack + element. Calib = Sum × (1 + affinity × 0.25), or × 0.75 on the negative share. Crit ATK — final affinity a = affinity + 30% (Critical Eye 1, Weakness Exploit 1). Critical Boost 5 hits attack and element alike: Sum × (1 + a × 0.50). Crit ELE — the elemental build, same affinity a. Critical Boost 5 and Critical Element 5 cannot realistically be worn together with the affinity skills, so this uses Critical Boost 1 (130%) with Critical Element 5 multiplying on top of it, on element only: the critting element share deals 1.30 × 1.80 = 2.34. ATK × (1 + a × 0.30) + ELE × a × 2.34 + ELE × (1 − a). CF ATK — final affinity affinity − 30%, no affinity skills alongside it. With n = |final affinity|, a negative crit deals 250% on a measured 30% proc and 75% otherwise, so Sum × ((1 − n) + n × 1.275). Best = the highest of those three builds, ignoring any that cannot apply. The table is sorted by it.
Blast, Poison, Paralysis and Sleep weapons carry an ELE value that is a status buildup rate, not damage — higher triggers the ailment sooner. Those 110 weapons are treated as no-element in every damage column, and their ELE is shown greyed with a †. For the same reason the Normal ammo filter excludes Poison / Paralysis / Sleep ammo, which the source types as NORMAL but which gains nothing from Normal Ammo Boost — those sit under Status.
