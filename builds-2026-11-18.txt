SET C IS THE SAME ARMOUR AS THE CHARGE BLADE FIGHT
--------------------------------------------------
Set C is piece for piece the set worn in 12-16-11 (Thanatos Force,
471,759) and 12-10-30 / 12-13-20 (Axe of Demons, 302,796 and 227,211):
Viper Kadachi helm, mail and coil, Rathian vambraces, Bazelgeuse
greaves. Power Prolonger 2 from the two driftsmelt slots, which also
confirms the level inferred from that fight's 53.7s Axe Boost window.

So the Hunting Horn run is directly comparable to those three, and the
Dual Blades run differs by one piece. That is the closest this project
has come to a controlled multi-class comparison.

BUT THESE ARE NOT MATCHED-ARMOUR COMPARISONS ACROSS THE BOARD, and it
would be wrong to treat them as one set of five. Sets A and B are
tailored to their weapons - Evading Reload 4 for the bowgun, Blunt Force
and Slugger for the hammer - so they answer "what does this player
achieve with each class" rather than "what is the class worth with
armour held constant". Both are worth knowing. They are different
questions and the second one is the one the engine gets wrong.

STILL MISSING
-------------
   - the driftsmelt STAT bonus on three pieces. The skill is readable on
     all of them (Viper Kadachi Helm: Artful Dodger 1, Somnacanth U
     Braces: Artful Dodger 1, Kushala Crus: Earplugs 1) but the number
     underneath is covered - by the phone's navigation bar on two, and
     by the game's own floating Equip button on the helm. These run +1
     to +6 attack, +1% affinity or +1 to +4 defence elsewhere in this
     set, so the exposure is small but not zero
   - the Switch Axe dummy weapon in 17-26-37, 17-28-35 and 17-30-26. No
     screenshot. Reported as 0 affinity, deliberately, so the damage is
     stable - which also makes it a DIFFERENT weapon from Axe of Demons
     (-30%), so its dummy figures cannot be compared to the Axe of
     Demons live fights without knowing its attack

engine still does not take from the data. On Set C that is two Power
Prolonger 1 slots stacking to Power Prolonger 2, plus Attack +6 and
Attack +4 - a skill level and 10 attack the engine scores as zero.
