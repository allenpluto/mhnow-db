Damage per round by ammo type: the first measurement
====================================================
Bazel Typhoon 5, Light Bowgun, Fire. Status screen: attack 2007, affinity
-30%, element 1668. Equipment skills: Defense Boost 2, Earplugs 2, Evading
Reload 2 (the weapon's own), Health Boost 1, Divine Blessing 1, Lock On 1,
Tremor Resistance 1, Special Insurance 1 - NO DAMAGE SKILLS AT ALL, which is
what makes the numbers comparable. Four training-dummy recordings,
2026-11-13, one magazine of each ammo type per recording.

WHY THIS WAS WORTH MEASURING
----------------------------
This engine scores every round the same. It knows a magazine's size, its
recoil and its reload, and from those it works out how FAST you can fire -
but it has always assumed each round is worth the same damage. On a bowgun
carrying normal, sticky and cluster that assumption is not slightly wrong.

READING THEM
------------
The magazine icons are fire-orange on this weapon, so the blue-weapon reader
does not apply; the discriminator is BRIGHT orange (R>210, R-B>120, G<170),
which keeps the loaded icon and drops the dark brown border an empty slot
leaves behind. The three magazines separate by geometry and fill:

   normal    5 slots, and the outer two boxes are reached by nothing else
   sticky    2 slots, narrow icons   about 36 bright pixels per round
   cluster   2 slots, FAT icons      about 105 per round

The Total Damage panel RESETS after a few seconds of no damage, which is the
trap in this recording set - and also the thing that made the reading
possible, because it separated the impact from the explosion.

THE READINGS
------------
   NORMAL    5282 / 5 HIT       5593 / 5 HIT          5 shots each
   STICKY      84 / 2 HIT         84 / 2 HIT    96 / 2 HIT
             6594 / 4 HIT *     6690 / 4 HIT
   CLUSTER   8493 / 4 HIT       8505 / 4 HIT          one shot
            16998 / 8 HIT      17010 / 8 HIT          two shots

   * read after the impact numbers had already reset away, leaving the two
     explosions alone - and 6594 / 2 is 3297, exactly the explosion figure
     the 6690 reading gives. The two agree to the unit.

DAMAGE PER ROUND
   normal     1088      one hit per shot
   sticky     3341      = 3297 explosion + 44 impact, two hits per shot
   cluster    8501      four hits per shot

A SHOT IS NOT A HIT, AND THE TWO PARTS BEHAVE DIFFERENTLY
---------------------------------------------------------
The player's account is confirmed exactly. A sticky or cluster round lands a
small IMPACT first and a large EXPLOSION after a delay, and they are not the
same kind of damage:

   the impact is 2 digits - 84, 84, 96 for two rounds, so 42 to 48 each -
      and it SCATTERS, because it is affinity-affected and this weapon sits
      at -30%
   the explosion is CONSISTENT. Four cluster readings give 8493, 8505, 8499
      and 8505: a spread of 0.14%. On a weapon where 30% of hits should be
      landing at 75%, that is not scatter at all, so the explosion does not
      take affinity.

Normal ammo behaves the opposite way, as it should: 1056 and 1119 per round
on the two runs, a 6% swing between two five-shot samples, which is the
affinity scatter showing through.

SO ON THIS BUILD, AFFINITY AND EVERY CRIT SKILL REACH ABOUT 1% OF A CLUSTER
ROUND'S DAMAGE - the impact only. The engine applies them to all of it.

DAMAGE PER SECOND, using the engine's own firing rates
------------------------------------------------------
                     no style     stepping shot   stepping + Evading Reload 4
      normal            863             818                       818
      sticky           1248            1572                      2051
      cluster          3174            4000                      5220

CLUSTER IS 6.4x NORMAL AMMO at their respective bests, on the weapon the
suggester currently ranks as a normal-ammo gun.

Note what stepping shot does here, and does not. It is worth +26% on the
2-round magazines, whose native interval is 2.056s, and it is a LOSS on
normal ammo, whose native 0.862s is quicker than the 0.95s dodge. Mixing
them is the worst of both.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
-------------------------
   - One weapon. The per-round figures are Bazel Typhoon's, and nothing here
     says how they scale with attack, or whether the ratio between ammo types
     holds on another bowgun.
   - Whether the explosion scales with attack at all. It plainly does not
     scale with affinity; if it is flat damage, then attack skills are worth
     far less on a cluster build than the engine thinks, and the right
     comparison changes again. One more recording at a very different attack
     value would settle it, and it is the single most valuable thing left to
     measure here.
   - A dummy has no hitzones and no element resistance. Sticky and cluster
     are usually fixed damage that ignores hitzones, which would make them
     BETTER on a real monster than this comparison suggests, not worse.

NOT MODELLED, DELIBERATELY. Pricing this properly needs a damage figure per
ammo family for every bowgun, and there is exactly one weapon's worth of data.
Inventing the rest would be worse than admitting the gap, so the suggester now
says plainly, on any bowgun carrying cluster or sticky, that its ranking
treats every round alike and is not to be trusted on the choice of magazine.
