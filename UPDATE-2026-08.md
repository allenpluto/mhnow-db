# Skill improvements — August 2026 balance update

Grouped by armour series, most improved first. 35 pieces across 15 series.

`Was` is the level before the update. `G8` and `G10` are the levels now, read
live from `data.json`. The buff is grade-tiered on the older monster sets, so
G8 and G10 often differ — the max-grade number alone hides that.
