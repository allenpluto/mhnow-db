Dual Blades: Demon Mode, styles, and what Power Prolonger buys
==============================================================

Method
------
Three training-dummy recordings, one per style, decoded at 20fps. The energy
bar under the health bar (x 150..332, y 944..958) is the state signal: it
drains while Demon Mode runs and refills while it does not. It is YELLOW
normally and RED under Feral Demon Mode, which is how the two are told apart -
a yellow-only test reads Feral as an empty bar. Damage comes from the dummy's
own Total Damage counter, read at each boundary.

The style is named in the dropdown at the top of the screen, so each recording
says which style it is without being told.

Energy, by style
----------------
   No style      drains 5.2%/s   full bar lasts 19.2s
                 refills 5.6%/s  back to full in 17.9s
   Feral Demon   drains 13.5%/s  full bar lasts 7.4s
                 refills ~6.4%/s
   Spiral Slash  drains ~5.5%/s, plus the Spiral Slash itself eats a large
                 chunk outright - the bar fell 74% to 0 in 1.5s during one

So Feral Demon burns energy 2.6 times faster than plain Demon Mode. That is
the trade the style is named for.

Damage per second, measured
---------------------------
   style          state              dmg/s   hits/s   dmg/hit
   No style       demon, standing     1732     3.26      557
   No style       demon, moving       1216     4.22      288
   No style       normal               985     2.53      389
   Feral Demon    feral demon         1369     2.62      523
   Feral Demon    normal              1912     3.33      574
   Spiral Slash   demon, standing     1392     3.19      436
   Spiral Slash   the Spiral Slash    1753     2.60      672
   Spiral Slash   normal               896     2.34      383

Three findings fall out.

DEMON MODE IS WORTH 1.76x NORMAL on No style, standing still. It gets there
from both directions: 43% more damage a hit and 29% more hits a second.

ATTACKING WHILE MOVING COSTS A THIRD. Demon Mode's moving attack lands MORE
often - 4.22 hits a second against 3.26 - but each hit is worth half as much,
288 against 557, so it nets 1216 against 1732. Movement is bought with damage,
not given free.

FERAL DEMON MODE LOSES TO ITS OWN NORMAL STANCE, 1369 against 1912. The user
predicted this from feel before it was measured. What makes it stranger is
that the Feral style's NORMAL stance is nearly twice No style's - 1912 against
985 - which is the single number here most worth re-checking, since the other
two styles agree with each other on normal stance (985 and 896) and only this
one does not.

What that makes of the whole cycle
----------------------------------
Attacking continuously and using Demon Mode whenever the bar allows:

   No style      demon up 19.2 / (19.2 + 17.9) = 52% of the time
                 cycle averages 1372 dmg/s, against 985 never using it  +39%
   Feral Demon   demon up 7.4 / (7.4 + 15.6) = 32%
                 cycle averages 1738 dmg/s, against 1912 NEVER USING IT

So on Feral Demon the best play looks like never entering Demon Mode at all.
That rests on the 1912 figure, so it rests on re-checking it.

Power Prolonger
---------------
NOT YET MEASURED - no recording carries it. But the shape of its value is now
fixed: it slows the drain, which lengthens Demon Mode, which raises the share
of the hunt spent at the higher damage. On No style, if it stretches the bar
by a factor k:

   k = 1.0   demon up 52%   1372 dmg/s   (baseline)
   k = 1.3   demon up 58%   1421 dmg/s   +3.6%
   k = 1.5   demon up 62%   1446 dmg/s   +5.4%

The engine currently prices Power Prolonger 3 at 15%. Unless it stretches the
bar by more than half, that is too generous for Dual Blades on these numbers -
though a dummy cannot see the other half of Demon Mode's worth, which is
attacking through a slide while a real monster is swinging at you.

It should also be worth MORE on Feral Demon than on No style, since Feral is
the style that runs out of energy. That is a prediction the recording can
check rather than an assumption.

What to record next
-------------------
One run, No style, Power Prolonger 3: enter Demon Mode on a full bar and
attack until it empties. The drain rate is the only unknown; the damage
figures above do not need repeating. A second run at Lv1 would say whether
the levels are linear, which Recoil Down taught us not to assume.
